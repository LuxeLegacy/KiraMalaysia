# CALCULATION FLOW

1. Calculate Total Reliefs
2. Compute Chargeable Income
   Gross Income – Total Reliefs
3. Apply progressive brackets (resident)
4. Apply flat 30% if non-resident
5. Compute:
   • Total Tax
   • Effective Tax Rate
   • Monthly PCB Estimate (Total Tax ÷ 12)
6. Deduct Zakat (if applicable)
7. Show Final Tax Payable

All logic must be modular and reusable.
